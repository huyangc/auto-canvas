## Goal

User decision 2026-11-14 ("Yes, fix the unit"): the frozen readability
`area` metric must not depend on a drawing's editor grid setting. Today
`readability-metrics.ts:106` divides the bounding-box area by
`document.presentation.grid²`. 621 corpus drawings use grid 10 and 7 use
grid 1 (dev `3ze5gasahn`, `dr4e8zhyjg`, `rxdzz3wdry`, `tr828v3mrc`; val
`qzszatannt`, `s78j9fyzha`, `vyqjt4w5gv`), so those 7 weigh about 100× more
in the summed composite, for human and result alike. Measuring area in fixed
10 × 10-unit squares leaves every grid-10 drawing unchanged and makes the
seven comparable. Opus's estimate on main's report (`tmp/grid-area.py`):
composite / human dev 1.271 → 1.247, val 1.244 → 1.208.

This is a units fix only. Weights and scales in `readability-weights.ts`
stay frozen (not refitted); other uses of `pitch` stay as they are.
