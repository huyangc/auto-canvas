| 1 | must-fix | `mcts-placement.ts` (`buildPlacements`) | Every device in a vertical DC branch is placed at raw symbol origin `position.x = snap(x)` (`mcts-placement.ts:335`). Because a built-in resistor/capacitor/source has conducting pins at local `x = 0` while a built-in MOS has conducting channel pins (`D`/`S`) at local `x = +10` (`mirror: "none"`) or `x = -10` (`mirror: "horizontal"`), stacking a resistor or current source with a MOS (or stacking opposite-facing MOS devices) in one column misaligns their conducting pins by 10–20 units. On `vhg3q4gax7`, `R1.2` lands at `x = 210` while `M2.S` lands at `x = 200`, causing a tiny Z-jog (`avoidableJogs: 0 → 1`) that adds `+5,123` to `readabilityTerm` and makes the 0-crossing MCTS masterpiece lose to 1-crossing `layered`! | Align every device `i` in a DC branch by its **conducting pin axis** in its chosen pose: compute `axisX` from the posed local `x` of `channel.drain` (for transistors) or the first conducting pin (for two-pin vertical parts), and set `position.x = snap(x - axisX)`. Every device in a column then has collinear conducting pins (`avoidableJogs = 0`). Also, when branches have unequal device counts (e.g. 3 devices `[R1, M2, M4]` vs 2 devices `[M1, M3]` in `vhg3q4gax7`), align devices by their potential tier / shared-gate partner row instead of linearly stretching `[0, lastRow]` so `M1` sits level with its mirror partner `M2`. Add a regression test in `mcts-placement.test.ts` verifying zero `avoidableJogs` (collinear conducting `D`/`S`/resistor pins) on a stacked resistor+PMOS+NMOS clamp-opamp circuit AND verifying `planAutoLayout` selects `"mcts"` with 0 crossings. |
| 2 | must-fix | `mcts-placement.ts` (`evaluateProxy`, `buildPlacements`) | On `m6rez5w83z` (MOS-degenerated differential pair with horizontal `M3`/`M4` bridges and external input ports `Vinp`/`Vinn`), MCTS correctly placed `M3`/`M4` horizontally in the center between `M1/I1` and `M2/I2`, but `M1` and `M2` faced **inward** (`M1` right, `M2` left), forcing external input ports `Vinp`/`Vinn` to wrap over `Voutp`/`Voutn` (2 avoidable crossings!). In addition, when multiple bridge devices stack in the same inter-column gap (`M3`/`M4`), the branch vertical row pitch (`pitch.member = 80`) is tight, pushing the lower bridge (`M4`) below the tail current sources (`I1`/`I2`). | (a) In `evaluateProxy`, include external port / `roles.byNet.get(net) === "input"` awareness for outer-column transistors: a transistor on the leftmost column (`col === 0`) whose gate is on an external port Net (or `"input"` Net) prefers facing outward left (`pinExit.x < 0`), and on the rightmost column (`col === count - 1`) prefers facing outward right (`pinExit.x > 0`) — count a gate-facing penalty when an outer-column port-driven gate faces inward across its own channel. (b) When two or more parallel bridge devices share the same inter-column midpoint (stack count >= 2), widen the vertical separation between the upper and lower branch rows around their endpoint pins (e.g., row pitch at least `120` when a multi-device bridge spans the branches) and center the vertical stack around the endpoint midpoint (`y + (stack - (total - 1) / 2) * 60`) so both bridges sit cleanly between the upper and lower branch devices. Add a regression test in `mcts-placement.test.ts` on the MOS-degenerated differential pair with input/output ports verifying outward-facing `M1`/`M2` gates, horizontal `M3`/`M4` bridges between the two columns, and `planAutoLayout` selecting `"mcts"` with 0 crossings. |
