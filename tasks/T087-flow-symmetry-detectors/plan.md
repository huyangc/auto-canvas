readability weights (planner **selection** weights unchanged; evaluator

- `wvwmyesa4g` (dev): a feedback amplifier whose signal flows right → left
  (`V_out` on the left, `V_in` in the middle). `backwardFlow` and `portOrder`
  have **zero opportunities** on both the human drawing and the result.

(never on planner recognition), measures them on human drawings and results
alike, and refits the evaluation weights so the readability goal sees these
defects. Planner selection and plans stay byte-identical to main.

## Steps

1. **Investigate.** Why `backwardFlow` and `portOrder` have no opportunity on
   `wvwmyesa4g` (port Net roles, signal distances), and why
   `symmetryTopology` finds no pair on `bv9zwmd835`. Report on these two and
   count, across all 628 human drawings, how many have zero flow and zero
   symmetry opportunities today.
2. **Signal-flow detector (`signalDirection`).** Infer direction from pin
   roles: for every multi-pin signal block or device with input and output
   pins, its input-side Nets precede its output-side Nets. Derive input /
   output ports from connectivity when their roles are unset: a port reached
   only from an amplifier output (through at most passive parts) is an
   output, one reaching an amplifier input is an input. Count, as
   opportunities, (input port, output port) pairs and (amplifier input pin,
   output pin) pairs; a violation is an output left of its input (x
   comparison of the elements' visible centres). Keep `backwardFlow` /
   `portOrder` as they are; this is a new metric.
3. **Netlist symmetry detector (`netlistSymmetry`).** Find a non-trivial
   involution of the connectivity graph: devices swapped with a partner of the
   same symbol and model, Nets mapped consistently, rails and self-mapped
   Nets fixed, at least one transistor pair in the component (so plain
   parallel parts never count alone). Deterministic, bounded search (state
   the bound). Opportunities: swapped pairs; violations: a pair that is not
   level (same y within one grid) or not mirrored about one common vertical
   axis per component (within one grid). Do not reuse `symmetryTopology`.
4. **Tests**: synthetic feedback amplifier drawn left → right vs right → left;
   degenerated differential pair mirrored vs not; parallel identical
   resistors alone do not form a pair; determinism. Synthetic circuits only.
5. **Refit** evaluation weights with the T086 pooled procedure (both layout
   sets remeasured with the new metric code). Report weights, discrimination
   on dev and val for both layout sets vs the T086 evaluation weights, the
   three flagged drawings, and the new goal baseline.
